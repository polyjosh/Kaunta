Kaunta
Design system · v0.1 · Foundations

Kaunta shows you when your till, your cash, and your shelf don’t agree.

The look is a well-lit counter at night: a warm dark ground, one ember accent for the next action, and status you can read by colour, icon and word together.

1 · Speed at the counter
Two or three taps to record a sale. No typing in the main flow.
2 · Readable in a dark, busy bar
Body 16px minimum, money much larger. Targets 48px minimum, 64px for main actions.
3 · Never ambiguous
Every status is colour + icon + label. Colour alone never carries meaning.
4 · Plain language, two languages
English and Kiswahili. Every label is laid out to take 30% more length.
5 · Visible accountability
Who served, who recorded and when ride along on every order, without extra steps.
6 · Offline is normal
A calm sync pill that is always visible. Offline is grey, never an alarm.

Ember is for actions only. It is never used as a status colour, so a bright orange button can’t be mistaken for “pending”.
Paid and Error differ in lightness too (mint vs coral), and Pending is blue rather than amber, so colour-blind staff still see the difference.
Contrast: every text token passes 4.5:1 on its own theme’s Night, Counter and Tile surfaces.

Type · Manrope

One family, four weights (500–800). Money always uses tabular figures so totals line up, and is always written KES 1,250: the prefix, comma thousands, no decimals. A shortfall is written as a word, “Short KES 300”, never as a bare minus sign.

48 is the floor for anything tappable. 56 is used for list rows and sheets, 64 for the main counter actions, and 72 for PIN keys. Tappable things sit at least 8px apart, so a wet thumb or a cracked screen doesn’t hit the wrong one.
