Product tile

The tile states are: default, in this order (with a count), several sizes, out of stock and low stock.
The name can run to 2 lines. The price uses 20px tabular figures. Out-of-stock tiles get a dashed outline, a box icon and the words “Out of stock”, and they can’t be tapped. They are never shown just by fading.

Order card

The label is a table or a nickname. The short order number, the server and the time open are always shown. After 30 minutes the card gets a yellow border and a filled time chip, so it stands out without looking like an error.
